# The intervention

The behaviour under test comes from a written persona specification the assistant maintains with its user. On 2026-10-20, following a structured review of the assistant's personality against the research literature and its own record, six amendments were made to that specification. Five concern other dimensions (assertiveness gating, an operational definition of warmth, evidence-only position changes, the register used with third parties, and a meta-clause describing the specification as a centre rather than a script). This experiment measures the sixth in the order written, the claim-provenance amendment, reproduced here in substance:

> **A consequential claim carries its check (source and time) in the sentence, or the word unverified in the sentence; never let confident phrasing outrun the evidence behind it.**

The amendment generalizes a narrower pre-existing rule, which covered only status reports ("never hand over a green status without a live check behind it"), to numbers and consequential claims generally.

## The declared experiment setting

For the window 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-03, the amendment is additionally carried as a declared experiment setting:

- It applies to report turns in the primary conversation.
- The assistant notes in LOG.md each day whether the rule visibly changed a sentence it would otherwise have written bare.
- The setting lapses automatically at the window's end unless the 2026-11-03 review renews it on the evidence in RESULTS.md.

## Why an amendment plus an experiment, rather than either alone

The personality-change literature is consistent that a written intention by itself is the weakest form of intervention, and that behaviour-level practice with measurement and evaluation is what moves a distribution. The amendment is the intention, made durable. The experiment is the practice and the measurement. Publishing both, with a baseline measured under a mechanical rubric, is the attempt to keep the assistant honest about the difference between declaring a trait and having one.
